# One rail for every setting

Settings had three levels of navigation: the Settings tab, six tabs across the top (General,
Terminal, SSH and SFTP, RDP, Security, Advanced), and a rail of sections inside each tab. This
release keeps only the rail.

## What you will see

- **One rail, every section.** The rail on the left lists all 26 sections under their six names,
  in the order the tabs had. Click a section, or move through them with the Up and Down keys; the
  names are headings, not stops. The rail scrolls on a short window and keeps the name of the open
  section's group in sight.
- **Sections on the page.** The rail and the open section sit directly on the window background;
  only the setting cards keep their own surface. The outer frame and the inner frame that used to
  wrap them are gone.
- **A glyph instead of a badge.** The line under each card title that said "Applies immediately,
  kept on Save", "Saved immediately, not by Save" or "Applies to connections opened after Save" is
  now a small glyph beside the title. Hover it to read the text; a screen reader reads the same
  text. The three glyphs are explained once, at the foot of the rail.
- **Scroll bar at the edge.** On a wide window, the scroll bar of a Settings section stopped two
  thirds of the way across and the rest of the window stayed empty. It now sits at the right edge
  of the window, and the cards fill the section width.
- **Reset RDP defaults** shows under the open section while an RDP section is open.

The full list is in [docs/CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).
