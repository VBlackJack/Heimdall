# A Command Library that fits a laptop screen, with a panel you can resize

This release comes from a usability review of the Command Library. On a laptop-sized window the
command detail took the whole tool and hid both the list and the Copy and Send buttons; two panels
could leave the detail impossible to bring back. Both are fixed, and the detail panel now has a grip
to choose its height.

## What you will see

- **The list and the buttons stay on screen.** In a short window the command detail no longer pushes
  the list or the Copy, Insert and Send buttons out of view: the list keeps room for two rows and
  the detail scrolls instead.
- **Resize the detail panel.** Drag the grip on top of the command detail, the history or the
  runbooks to give it the height you want, or focus the grip and use the Up and Down arrows.
  Double-click the grip to go back to the automatic height. Heimdall remembers the height you chose.
- **The command detail comes back after the history or the runbooks.** Closing either panel shows
  the command you had selected again; before, the row stayed highlighted over an empty space and
  clicking it did nothing.
- **Replaying a history row works for the command already selected.** It used to do nothing at all
  in that case, which was the most common one: send a command, open the history, replay it.
- **History rows use the whole width.** The time, Copy and Replay buttons of a history row now sit
  at the right edge of the panel instead of the middle.
