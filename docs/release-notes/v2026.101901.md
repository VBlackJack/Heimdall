# A settings panel you can find your way around

This release reorganizes Settings so that a setting is one click away instead of a long scroll,
and fixes what a pass over every section brought to light.

## Settings, section by section

- **Sections listed down the left.** Every Settings tab with more than one section lists them on
  the left and shows the open one on the right. General and Security, one long page each until
  now, are split into six sections each. SSH & SFTP, RDP and Advanced keep their sections, now in
  the same list.
- **Keyboard.** Tab enters the list once, and the arrow keys move from one section to the next.
- **Nothing to relearn.** Settings search, the jump to a field in error and the links of the
  security overview all open the right section.
- **Command library sync** has its own section under Advanced, apart from the external tools.
- Every section takes the same width, and its cards stand out from the background.

## Easier to read

- **Errors show where they are.** A section with a field in error shows a red count, like its tab,
  and a screen reader hears it.
- **Switched-off settings look switched off.** Settings that only apply while a switch is on are
  dimmed as a whole when it is off: credential provider, Git sync, session logging, update checks,
  Windows Hello, SSH auto-reconnect and vault auto-lock. Only the boxes were dimmed before, and an
  empty dimmed box looked like an enabled one.
- The search boxes above the trusted host keys and the trusted certificates say what they search.
- The buttons beside those lists keep their size instead of stretching into the text beside them,
  and the lists are taller before they scroll.
- The Sysinternals, NirSoft and NanaRun folders have their label above the field, like every other
  field.
- **Reset RDP defaults** sits under the RDP settings instead of under the section list.

The guides that name a place in Settings now give its section. The full list is in
[docs/CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).
