# Clearer Settings sections, and the last contrast gaps closed

This release finishes the contrast work of v2026.102601. A second measurement of every theme, on
screen and in the code, found a few places that still fell short; they are fixed. The Settings menu
also groups its sections more clearly, and a refused sign-in through a plink tunnel is now named
correctly on a busy computer.

## What you will see

- **Settings sections stand out.** The group titles of the Settings menu (General, Terminal, SSH &
  SFTP, RDP and the others) are in capitals with more space above them, and the items under each
  title are indented, so the groups read as groups.
- **Input outlines are visible on Dracula.** The outlines of text fields, lists, check boxes and
  secondary buttons were hard to see on the Settings cards of the Dracula theme; they now stand out
  on every theme.
- **Warning and error outlines are easier to see.** The warning borders of the server key and FTPS
  certificate prompts, a field in error, danger buttons and status marks now use the readable status
  shades.
- **The pressed outline of a primary button shows.** Pressing a primary button now draws its pressed
  outline instead of the focus border.
- **RDP import status labels are no longer cut.** In the RDP import dialog, a long gateway or
  conflict label wraps inside its chip instead of being cut at the column edge.
- **Terminal scrollbar and loading text follow the colour scheme.** They were always drawn in fixed
  Dracula colours; on a light scheme the loading text was nearly white on white.
- **A refused plink sign-in is named correctly.** On a busy computer, a tunnel whose sign-in was
  refused could report that its port did not open. It now reports the refused sign-in.
- **Smaller fixes.** Some secondary text on the Parchment theme, the JWT parser's header, payload and
  signature colours, and the health dot of the RDP toolbar all read better.
