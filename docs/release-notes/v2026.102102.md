# Tool icons back, text you can read on every theme, and dialogs that behave like dialogs

This release is the result of a graphic pass over the whole interface. The sixty tool cards get
their icons back, every hint, error and accent text reads at the contrast the accessibility rules
ask for on all seventeen themes, region lines are visible on the light themes, modal dialogs lose
their Minimize and Maximize buttons, and the Tunnels and Scheduled tabs get the same empty state as
the Sessions tab.

## What you will see

- **Tool icons.** The cards of the Tools tab showed an empty tile where their icon should be: the
  icon was drawn at the opacity of the tint behind it. All sixty cards now show their icon, and
  SNMP Walker, whose icon was drawn with lines only, has a filled glyph.
- **Readable hints.** "F1 for shortcuts", "Network tools will use the selected target", "Pin your
  favorite tools" and twenty other hints were painted with the colour of disabled controls, at
  3.3 to 1 on the default theme and 2.4 to 1 on the light ones. They now use the secondary text
  colour, 7.5 to 1 and 6.3 to 1.
- **Error, warning, success, info and accent text on cards.** Red text on a card measured
  2.9 to 1 on Drakul; accent text on a card 3.8 to 1. The text colours are now derived from each
  theme so that every one reads at 4.5 to 1 on the window and on a card, and stays the theme's own
  colour wherever it already did. The Delete button of the detail pane, "Close All" in Tunnels,
  "Stop" in Port Scanner and the inline validation errors of every dialog follow.
- **Lines you can see.** The divider beside the session list, the rules under tool headers and
  above tool footers, the separators of grid headers and the outline of dialog section cards
  measured 1.6 to 1 on Drakul and 1.2 to 1 on Parchment and Folio. They now reach 3 to 1 on every
  theme. The faint outline of ordinary cards is unchanged.
- **Modal dialogs.** Edit Session, Gateway, Macro editor and twenty-two other dialogs showed
  Minimize and Maximize buttons; a minimized dialog left the main window disabled with nothing on
  screen. The buttons are gone; the dialogs keep their icon, their title and their resize border.
- **Drop-down lists that fit their value.** "Operating system of the far end" in Edit Session
  cut its own selected value; 37 drop-down lists now grow to the longest of their English, French
  and Spanish items instead of a fixed width.
- **Empty states.** Tunnels and Scheduled show the same card as Sessions: an icon, a title, a hint
  and the action. The Scheduled tab no longer shows an empty row with a lone check box above its
  list.
- **Narrow windows.** At the minimum window size the header of a terminal session trimmed its
  subtitle to a single letter; it now ends with an ellipsis. The file pane beside a local shell or
  an SFTP session showed a horizontal scrollbar even when its columns fitted.
- **First launch.** The welcome tour card stood over the session list it was pointing at, and a
  slice of the empty-state card showed beside it; the card now sits beside its target. At startup
  the keyboard focus goes to the last selected session or to the search box, so the whole sidebar
  is no longer framed.
- **Port Scanner** counters read "Open: 0" instead of "Open:: 0".
- **Light themes.** The veil behind a loading or connecting overlay follows the theme instead of
  being black on every theme.

## Under the hood

- Every font size in the interface is a typography or icon token, guarded by a test, and the
  spacing scale gains its 2 and 16 pixel steps.
- The derived colours (`ErrorTextBrush`, `WarningTextBrush`, `SuccessTextBrush`, `InfoTextBrush`,
  `AccentTextBrush`, `BorderStrongBrush`) are recomputed on every theme or accent change and
  tested against the seventeen themes; the accessibility checklist in `docs/` describes when each
  is used.

The checklist is in [docs/UX_ACCESSIBILITY_CHECKLIST.md](https://github.com/VBlackJack/Heimdall/blob/v2026.102102/docs/UX_ACCESSIBILITY_CHECKLIST.md) and the
full list is in [docs/CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/VBlackJack/Heimdall/blob/v2026.102102/docs/CHANGELOG.md).
